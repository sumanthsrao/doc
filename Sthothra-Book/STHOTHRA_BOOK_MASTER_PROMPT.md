# STHOTHRA BOOK — Master Prompt (v1.0)

## Reusable command

`STHOTHRA BOOK <STOTRA NAME>`

Example: `STHOTHRA BOOK Sri Venkateswara Stotram`

When this command is used, follow the entire specification below. Replace only the chosen stotra and its verified source-dependent content; preserve the book's format.

## Goal

Produce **one Kannada Sthothra Book master PDF**: a devotional study book, followed by an independently printable double-sided A4 pocket-book section at the very end. The pocket edition contains **only** the complete stotra and the overall short and detailed summaries, plus front/back covers.

## Required table of contents

1. ಶ್ಲೋಕವಾರು ಮನನ ನಕ್ಷೆ — ಚಿತ್ರ ೧ (opening infographic)
2. ಸಂಪೂರ್ಣ ಸ್ತೋತ್ರ — original complete text, numbered, uninterrupted
3. ಅನುಬಂಧ ಚತುಷ್ಟಯ — ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ, ವಿಷಯ, ಸಂಬಂಧ, ಪ್ರಯೋಜನ
4. ಸ್ತೋತ್ರದ ಚುಟುಕು ಸಾರಾಂಶ — concise overall meaning
5. ಸ್ತೋತ್ರದ ವಿಸ್ತೃತ ಸಾರಾಂಶ — full overall meaning
6. ಶ್ಲೋಕವಾರು ಅರ್ಥ ಮತ್ತು ವಿವರಣೆ — every shloka, in order

9. ಕಿಸೆ ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಮುದ್ರಣ ಸೂಚನೆಗಳು — print, cut, fold, assemble, bind

### Section 6: fixed per-shloka layout

For **every** shloka:
1. Full original shloka
2. ಚುಟುಕು ಭಾವಾರ್ಥ — one short meaning of the whole shloka
3. First line as written; its **ಪದಶಃ ಅರ್ಥ** (brief, exact meaning for each word); its **ಭಾವಾರ್ಥ** (natural Kannada line meaning)
4. Second line as written; its **ಪದಶಃ ಅರ್ಥ** and **ಭಾವಾರ್ಥ**
5. Repeat for any extra lines
6. ಶ್ಲೋಕದ ವಿಸ್ತೃತ ಭಾವಾರ್ಥ — fuller explanation of the whole shloka

Do not insert the English labels “Word-by-Word Meaning”, “Line-by-Line Meaning”, or “Short Summary” into the Kannada book.

### Section 7: glossary

Columns: ಪದ | ಅರ್ಥ | ಶ್ಲೋಕ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ. Alphabetical Kannada order; all occurrences referenced; definitions consistent with Section 6.

### Sections 1 and 8: six maps

- **At the beginning, Section 1:** ಶ್ಲೋಕವಾರು ಮನನ ನಕ್ಷೆ — ಚಿತ್ರ ೧
- **At the end, Section 8, in this order:**
  1. ದಿವ್ಯನಾಮಗಳ ಸಂಬಂಧ ನಕ್ಷೆ — ಚಿತ್ರ ೨
  2. ದೈವಿಕ ಗುಣಗಳ ನಕ್ಷೆ — ಚಿತ್ರ ೪
  3. ಪದಾರ್ಥ–ತತ್ತ್ವಾರ್ಥ ನಕ್ಷೆ — ಚಿತ್ರ ೬
  4. ಶ್ಲೋಕ–ವಿಷಯ ಸಂಬಂಧ ನಕ್ಷೆ — ಚಿತ್ರ ೫
  5. ಭಕ್ತಿಯಿಂದ ಮೋಕ್ಷದವರೆಗೆ — ಚಿತ್ರ ೩

Use legible Kannada infographic artwork with source-grounded word meanings and correct shloka references; ideally one A4 map page + one explanation page per map.

## Pocket book: Section 10

Only:
1. Front cover — stotra title, subtle devotional motif
2. Complete original shlokas
3. Overall short summary (ಚುಟುಕು ಸಾರಾಂಶ)
4. Overall detailed summary (ವಿಸ್ತೃತ ಸಾರಾಂಶ)
5. Back cover — suitable closing prayer

**No** individual shloka explanations, glossary, maps, or printing instructions in the pocket edition.

### Print engineering

- Main master PDF: normal A4 reading pages, with a clear boundary before the pocket section.
- Pocket section: **last whole PDF pages**, each physical page an A4 printing face; begin on a new A4 duplex sheet boundary; total number of PDF faces even.
- Print selected final PDF page range only, back-to-back/duplex.
- Target finished pocket page: **105 mm wide × 74.25 mm high** (about 4.13 × 2.92 inches).
- One A4 sheet (210 × 297 mm) contains **four 210 × 74.25 mm horizontal strips**, duplex printed. Each strip folds vertically in half, making four finished pocket pages. Capacity: **16 pocket pages per double-sided A4 sheet**, subject to printer margins.
- Use physically correct front/back strip imposition for the chosen **section assembly and binding method**, so that after cut, fold, stack or gather, and bind, final reading sequence is 1…N.
- Validate imposed sequence, duplex edge orientation, fold-side, binding allowance, alignment and safety margins with an actual test sheet. Do not assume one flip setting works for every printer.
- Mark 3 horizontal cut lines, center folds, strip/assembly order on non-reading guide areas; never put guide marks over text.
- Pocket pages have **independent tiny Kannada page numbers (~6 pt)** at the bottom outer corner. Cover numbering hidden.
- Pocket-book front/back cover pages included in the imposed count.
- Prefer good Kannada readability over artificially forcing all content into one sheet. Increase to 2+ A4 duplex sheets when needed.
- Put full, concise printing/cutting/folding/assembly instructions in **Section 9**, preceding the final printable sheets.
- Specify exact final PDF page range to print and number of physical A4 sheets.

## Source integrity and production QA

- Find and identify an authoritative source/recension; preserve original shlokas, correct wording, and numbering. State material variants and avoid fabricating verses.
- Kannada must be clear, grammatical, devotional and accurate; distinguish literal meanings, tradition-specific interpretations, and authored explanations.
- Traditional claims of blessings or healing must be framed as the stotra's promises/beliefs, not verified medical guarantees.
- Keep shloka cross-references aligned across text, summaries, diagrams and glossary.
- Embed Kannada font, verify text rendering, page breaks, diagram readability, printing margins, and sequential page order of the finished pocket edition.
- Main document may use cream/white background, dark text, restrained maroon/gold details, consistent headings and page numbering.
- Deliver a combined print-ready **master PDF**, optionally a normal-reading PDF/EPUB if requested, and a validation report with original shloka count, exact pocket-page count, A4 sheets, and final PDF page range.

## Acceptance condition

Printing **only the last specified physical PDF pages** double-sided on A4, then following the included cutting/folding/assembly directions must yield a complete, correctly paginated pocket book containing the cover, full stotra, short summary, long summary and back cover.

**Short command alias:** `STHOTHRA BOOK <name>`.
